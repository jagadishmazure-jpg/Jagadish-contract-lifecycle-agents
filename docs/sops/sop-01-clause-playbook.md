# SOP-01: Clause Review Playbook

## Purpose
Every clause in a new or renewing contract is scored against the clause
playbook before legal review. The scorer cites the exact rule that fired.

## Playbook rules
Liability caps must be mutual and at least twelve months of fees
(CLAUSE-LIAB-01). One-sided caps, or caps below six months, are rejected
at triage. Termination for convenience requires at least ninety days
notice, and cure periods must be at least thirty days (CLAUSE-TERM-02).

## Auto-renewal handling
Auto-renewal clauses must give at least sixty days opt-out notice
(CLAUSE-RENEW-03). Shorter windows are flagged high risk and routed to
legal; the renewal owner is notified at twice the notice period.

## Indemnity and payment
Indemnity must be mutual and capped (CLAUSE-INDEM-04). Uncapped or
one-sided indemnity is never accepted without a T3 confirmation gate.
Payment terms beyond Net-60 need finance sign-off (CLAUSE-PAY-05).

## Governing law and confidentiality
Governing law must be an approved jurisdiction: Delaware, New York,
England, or Singapore (CLAUSE-GOV-06). Confidentiality should be mutual;
one-sided confidentiality is flagged (CLAUSE-CONF-07).
